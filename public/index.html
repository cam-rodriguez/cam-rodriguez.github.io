about

Cam Rodriguez is a data and investigative journalist based out of Chicago, Illinois. She combines traditional shoeleather reporting skills, public records requests, code and data analysis to find patterns lurking in documents and databases.

Right now, she's a data reporter at Chicago Public Media, which publishes The Chicago Sun-Times and WBEZ, Chicago's NPR station. There, she works on stories about everything under the sun, in print, on the web and on air. Before that, she was a Local Investigations Fellow at The New York Times.

Reach out at crodriguez@wbez.org or heycamrodriguez@gmail.com.
